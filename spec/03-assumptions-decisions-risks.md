## 1. How to use

- An **Assumption** is something the design relies on and nobody has proven. It has a validation method and a date by which it either becomes a claim ([02](02-source-register.md)) or triggers a redesign.
- An **Open Decision** is a choice that is the company's to make. Each shows options, a recommendation with reasons, the decider, and what it blocks. "Recommendation" is the architecture team's position, not a decision.
- Dates are Phase 0 weeks (W1 = first week). See [07](07-delivery-plan.md).
