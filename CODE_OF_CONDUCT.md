# Code of conduct

We want PayTrue to be a place where people can do careful, technical work together. This code applies to the repository, its issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where someone
represents the project.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Be precise and honest, especially about security and about what has and has not been tested. Do not overstate; correct yourself when you are wrong.
- Welcome newcomers; explain instead of dismissing. Review the work, not the author.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish someone's personal information, private messages, or the identity of a security reporter or a wallet-lab participant without their consent.
- Keep discussion on the project's work.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, or sexualised language or imagery.
- Doxxing, stalking, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Trolling, spam, and advertising of unrelated products or tokens. This is not a place to promote investments or to give financial advice.
- Using the project's channels to solicit or coordinate anything unlawful, including fraud or the theft of funds.
- Pressure to disclose a vulnerability before the reporter and maintainers agree on a date.

## Enforcement

The maintainers ([GOVERNANCE](GOVERNANCE.md)) may edit, hide or remove contributions that break this code, warn a person, or block them from the repository, and will explain the decision to the
person concerned. Decisions that affect a maintainer are taken by the other maintainers; where there is only one, the owner asks a trusted outside reviewer.

## Reporting

Report a violation privately to the maintainers. Until a dedicated address is published, use GitHub's private reporting form
(<https://github.com/MohAsadi/PayTrue/security/advisories/new>) and start the title with "Conduct report"; do not post details in public.

> **Owner action:** publish a monitored contact address for conduct reports before the first external release, and enable private vulnerability reporting (see [SECURITY](SECURITY.md)).

Reports are treated as confidential. A person who reports in good faith will not be punished for it.

## Attribution
