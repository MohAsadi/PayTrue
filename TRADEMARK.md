# Trademark policy

## Status

"PayTrue" is the name of this project. **No trademark registration is claimed by this repository, and no trademark clearance or search has been done.** The name may be in use by others, and
the same was noted for the earlier working name FastPay. This policy states the project's intent about the name; it is not a statement that the name is registered or available, and it does not
replace advice from a trademark lawyer. **Owner action:** clear the name and the domain before a public launch.

## The code is open; the name is not part of the license

The software and documentation are licensed under the Apache License 2.0. **That license does not grant any right to use the name "PayTrue", the name "PayTrue Cloud", or any logo of the
project** (Apache-2.0, section 6). You may use, modify, host, fork and sell services built on the code without asking.

## What you may do without asking

- Say that your product or service **uses, is built on, or is compatible with** PayTrue, when that is true, in plain text and without a logo.
- Refer to the project by name in documentation, articles, talks and issue reports.
- Distribute an **unmodified** copy of the repository or of its packages under the name they already have.
- Keep the names `@paytrue/*` in a fork that you keep in sync and describe as a fork ("a fork of PayTrue").

## What you may not do without permission

- Use "PayTrue" (or a confusingly similar name) as, or inside, the **name of your company, product, service, token, domain or social account** in a way that suggests it is the official project or endorsed by it.
- Distribute a **modified** version under the PayTrue name. Rename it; keep the license and notices ([NOTICE](NOTICE)).
- Use "PayTrue Cloud" for anything that is not a service operated by the project.
- Claim that your deployment is "official", "canonical", "audited by PayTrue", "approved by PayTrue" or "PayTrue certified". **No canonical deployment exists** ([contract deployments](docs/contracts-deployments.md)); a deployment you made is a *sovereign* one and must be described as yours.
- Claim that a wallet, a chain or a component is supported by PayTrue. Support is a statement backed by wallet-lab evidence that does not exist yet.

## Forks and operators

A fork that is a product should choose its own name and say "based on PayTrue". An operator of a self-hosted deployment may say "powered by PayTrue software" and must make clear that they, not the project,
run and are responsible for their service.

## Logos

The project has **no logo** at this time. If one is created, this policy will say how it may be used.

## Asking

To ask for permission or to report misuse, open an issue titled "Trademark" (no private details), or use the contact the owner publishes under [SECURITY](SECURITY.md) for sensitive matters.
